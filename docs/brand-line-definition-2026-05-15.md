# inryokü / inRYOKU Brand Line Definition

## Core Update

50% and 101% are not good vs bad.
They are not right vs wrong.
They are not a moral choice.

They are two brand lines, two states, two doors.

## Lines

| Line | Mark | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 50% | `inRYOKU` | shadow line / dense world / gravity / black-side presence |
| 101% | `inryoku` | light line / released world / expansion / color-side presence |

## Language Rule

Do not frame P2 as “which one do you choose?”.
Do not frame 50% and 101% as better/worse.
Do not over-explain the concept with observation language.

P2 can show two presences, but the copy should stay silent or minimal.
The user does not need to be told to choose.

## Design Implication

P2 should feel like standing between two brand gravities:

- `inRYOKU` = 50% line
- `inryoku` = 101% line

The experience should imply duality without turning it into a binary moral decision.
